Data & Methodology

The dataset was collected through a field survey around Naini Lake, Nainital, using geotagged observations of dustbin locations. After data cleaning, 54 valid dustbin locations were retained for spatial analysis.

The map uses point locations to visualize the distribution of surveyed dustbins, while the heatmap highlights areas with a higher concentration of mapped observations. Dustbin type is used as a categorical filter for exploring spatial patterns.
